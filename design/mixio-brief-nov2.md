# mix.io — state as of Nov 2, 2026 (latest)

Live at mix-io.github.io (home-screen PWA on iPhone) + a copy inside the Claude app. Owner: Alec. Read design/mixio-brief.md for the core game; this file is what changed.

Renames: the 0–100 ranking score is now called **eye**. **Chroma** is now a spendable currency (earned per level: tier 1–4 + accuracy bonus 0/+1/+2/+4, 0 under 50%, daily ×2).

New since the first brief:
- Bottom nav: home · play · atlas · collection · you. Liquid-glass bar with a draggable bubble that tracks the finger 1:1 (same spring as the collection top pill).
- Collection tab: packs / cards / passport, swipeable pages + draggable glass pill.
  - Base Set: 50 color cards (standard 30, holo 12, liquid 6 incl. 3 full-art scenes, gold prism 2) + secret rare 51/50 (Quebec blueberry, night scene). Packs: Standard 15 / Rare 40 / Premium 100 chroma, 3 cards, rarest last, face-up reveal with slicing + per-rarity effects over the home liquid swirl. Keep or sell (sell values std 2, holo 5, liquid 10, gold 30, secret 100). Cards rotate 360° by drag. Odds shown as donut charts. Odds/sell values still placeholders (unapproved).
  - Passport: a swipe-only 3D book — cover, identity page (DNA "photo", level, eye, stats, MRZ strip), country stamp pages (inked stamps, gold when complete).
- Complete a country: all colors of a 3+ color country found → gold light on the night globe, gold stamp, quiet moment.
- You tab: eye orb, 2×2 hero stats (world rank, level, countries, chroma), rank meter, world leaderboard (top 5 + you), top 12 postcards (best scores) + "all postcards" grouped by country flag.
- Onboarding: darker neutral welcome; player must stir the water to fill/unlock "begin"; color-DNA reveal is a big-bang animation.
- Removed/hidden: journey line on atlas, milestones & sets links (data still exists), the old journal tabs.

Alec's taste / rules: liquid, smooth, satisfying, cohesive, nothing that looks AI/templated; don't change the DNA blob/orb look; speed matters; nothing gets built without his approval on a board.
